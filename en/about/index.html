About golfspain.guide

An independent, no-nonsense directory of real membership fees and green fees at golf clubs across Spain.

What this site is

Finding out how much it actually costs to join a golf club in Spain — or to play as a visitor — is surprisingly difficult. Club websites are often vague, outdated, or simply don't publish their prices.

golfspain.guide exists to fix that. We research, verify, and publish real membership fees and green fees for golf clubs in Spain, so you can compare options before making a call or visiting in person.

How we verify prices

Prices are sourced directly from official club documents (PDF tariff sheets, published rate cards), club websites, or confirmed by phone with club staff. Every page shows a "last verified" date so you know how fresh the data is.

We do not publish guesses or estimates without clearly marking them as unverified. If a price is marked "verifying" or "TBC", it means we have not yet been able to confirm it from an official source.

Independence

This site has no commercial relationship with any golf club. We do not accept sponsorships, paid placements, or affiliate commissions. Clubs are listed because they exist and serve golfers — not because they pay us. Our only goal is to give golfers accurate, useful information.

Corrections & updates

Prices change. If you spot an error, an outdated price, or a club we're missing, please let us know. We update listings as quickly as we can verify new information.

You can reach us at: hello@golfspain.guide

If you're a club representative and want to update your listing, please get in touch with official documentation and we'll prioritise the update.

Coverage

We're starting with Valencia province — 9 clubs, ranging from the €10 green fee at Requena to the invitation-only Escorpión. We'll expand to other regions as we can verify data to the same standard.
