Real Club de Golf El Prat — Green Fees, Membership Cost & Visitor Guide 2026

45 Greg Norman holes, 30 minutes from Barcelona. Here's exactly what you'll pay, what to expect, and how to book.

Quick Answer

Green fees for visitors start at €108 weekday / €126 weekend in low season (Jan–Feb, Jul–Aug, Dec), rising to €138 weekday / €156 weekend in high season (Apr–May, Sep–Oct). Buggy not included.

Membership pricing is not published publicly — the club handles it by personal enquiry via admisiones@rcgep.com or +34 937 281 000. Individual, family, junior, senior, and 1-year formulas all available.

About Real Club de Golf El Prat

Founded in 1912 and granted Royal status by King Alfonso XIII, Real Club de Golf El Prat is one of Spain's most prestigious golf clubs. Located in Terrassa, about 30–35 minutes from Barcelona city centre, it's the only Greg Norman-designed course in Continental Europe and has hosted the Spanish Open ten times, most recently as a DP World Tour venue for the Estrella Damm Catalunya Championship.

The 45 holes are split into five 9-hole loops, combinable into two main courses: OPEN (the tournament layout) and BLUE (a slightly more accessible layout). Maximum handicap: 36 for men and women.

How to Book as a Visitor

Advance booking is essential — walk-ins are rarely possible, particularly on weekends.

  • Online: open.teeone.golf/en/prat
  • Phone: +34 937 281 000
  • Email: reservations@rcgep.com
⚠️ Dress code enforced. Collared shirts and golf trousers or shorts required. Denim and trainers not permitted.

Membership Cost — What We Know

Real Club de Golf El Prat doesn't list membership prices on its website. Pricing is handled through a personal meeting with the admissions team. What is confirmed publicly:

  • Individual membership
  • Family membership — covers partner and children; junior access to MiniAugusta academy (150+ children)
  • Junior / senior formulas
  • 1-year membership — trial option with Welcome Pack, available for individuals, couples, and families

As a benchmark: comparable Barcelona-area private clubs (PGA Catalunya, Club de Golf Barcelona) typically carry annual fees in the range of €2,000–€5,000+ plus an entry fee. El Prat should be treated as a similar tier.

The Courses — 45 Holes Explained

OPEN Course

The tournament course — Abajo and Arriba nines. DP World Tour standard. Par 72, 6,401m. The fairways punish wayward drives and the greens are large and undulating. El Prat's most demanding layout.

BLUE Course

More accessible, combining two of the other nines. Same Greg Norman design language, better suited to mid-to-high handicappers or first-time visitors. Par 72.

Facilities

  • Driving range — 300m, 50 tees (18 covered)
  • Practice areas — putting green, pitching green, chipping green, practice bunkers
  • Restaurant & snack bar
  • Pro shop, buggy, trolley & club hire
  • Pool area (members and guests)
  • Padel tennis courts
  • Wellness centre & changing rooms
  • Hotel La Mola — 185-room hotel directly adjacent, ideal for Stay & Play packages

Getting There

El Prat is in Terrassa, not El Prat de Llobregat — the name confuses first-time visitors. Plans de Bonvilar, 17, 08227 Terrassa. About 35 minutes from Barcelona city centre by car (A-2 or C-58 motorway) and 25 minutes from El Prat Airport. Parking on site is free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can visitors play at Real Club de Golf El Prat?

Yes. El Prat welcomes visitors every day, but advance booking is essential. Maximum handicap 36. Walk-ins without a reservation are generally not accepted, especially on weekends.

What is the cheapest time to play El Prat?

Low season (January–February, July–August, December) with a weekday tee time: €108. A mid-week round in January or August represents the best value at this course.

How much does membership cost at El Prat?

The club doesn't publish membership prices. Based on comparable Barcelona-area private clubs, expect annual fees of €2,000–€5,000+ with an additional entry fee. Contact admisiones@rcgep.com for a current quote.

Is there a 1-year or trial membership?

Yes. El Prat offers a 1-year membership with a Welcome Pack for individuals, couples, and families.

Does El Prat have a junior academy?

Yes — MiniAugusta is El Prat's junior golf school with over 150 children enrolled. Family memberships include access to the academy.
