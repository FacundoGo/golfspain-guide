Bonos and memberships: how to pay even less

If you are based in Valencia and playing more than twice a month, a bono or monthly membership will always beat the standard green fee rate. Here are the best-value options currently available.

Manises — €30 per round (9 holes, weekday)

The most affordable verified per-round rate in Valencia. A 10-round weekday bono for 9 holes costs €300, working out to €30 per round. Manises is a private club with a waiting list for membership, but visitors are welcome on weekdays (max handicap 36.0, booking required one day ahead).

Ifach summer promo — €39 for 18 holes

In July and August, Club de Golf Ifach offers an online promo rate of €39 for 18 holes between 14:00 and 17:00. Book via the club's online system. Standard visitor rate for 18 holes is €49.50 year-round.

Foressos 10-round bono — €65 per round

A 10-round weekday bono at Foressos Golf (18 holes) costs €650 total — €65 per round versus the standard €70. The weekend bono costs €700 (€70/round). For players who visit regularly, this is the most practical option without committing to a monthly membership.

Foressos afternoon membership — €155/month

For players who can play on weekday or weekend afternoons from 13:00, Foressos Golf offers an afternoon membership (Abono Tardes) for €155/month. There is no entry fee. At 3 rounds per week, this works out to under €13 per round — the lowest effective cost of any option in Valencia with no play restrictions.

Foressos weekday membership — €185/month

Full weekday access (Monday–Friday, any time) for €185/month with no entry fee. At 3 rounds per week, roughly €15 per round.

Course-by-course breakdown

Club de Golf Ifach — from €49.50

9 holes, Par 30, Benissa (Alicante). Designed by Javier Arana in 1974. One of the most affordable visitor experiences in the region and an underrated course. Visitors can play two 9-hole loops to complete an 18-hole equivalent round. Junior green fee (18H): €29.50.

Real Club de Golf Manises — from €57 (weekday 18H)

18 holes, Manises (Valencia). Private club with a waiting list, but open to visitors on weekdays. Maximum handicap 36.0 — booking required one day in advance by phone or email. The best-value 18-hole course in central Valencia. Weekend rate: €68.

Foressos Golf — from €70 (weekday 18H)

18 holes, Par 72, Picassent (Valencia). Open to all visitors, no membership required. Generous range of bonos and monthly memberships for regular players. Buggy available: €30. Driving range pass (60 balls): €6.

La Galiana Golf Resort — from €80

18 holes, Par 72, La Barraca de Aguas Vivas (Valencia). World Golf Awards 2019 Best Course Spain. Pay-and-play — no membership required. Seasonal pricing: €80 (winter/December) / €85 (summer, Jun 15–Sep 20) / €105 (shoulder seasons).

El Saler (Parador) — from €115

18 holes, Par 72, Valencia. Designed by Javier Arana in 1968. Regularly listed among the top 10 courses in continental Europe. The most expensive on this list but accessible to any visitor at the Parador's advance booking rate of €115. Rack rate: €150.

Which option is right for you?

Visiting once or twice: pay the standard green fee at Ifach (€49.50) or Manises (€57 weekday). Both are excellent value and no booking complexity.
Visiting several times on a trip: buy a Foressos bono. €650 for 10 rounds means you can play Monday to Friday across multiple weeks with no further admin.
Living locally, playing 2–3x per week: the Foressos afternoon membership at €155/month is the best-value option in the region. No entry fee, no long-term lock-in, and access to a well-maintained 18-hole course.
Serious golfer, long-term resident: explore membership at Manises (waiting list, €4,000 entry fee) or Ifach (transferable acción). Both offer excellent courses and full club membership benefits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest golf course in Valencia?

Club de Golf Ifach in Benissa (Alicante) offers the lowest standard visitor green fee in the region: €49.50 for 18 holes year-round (played as two 9-hole loops). In July and August, an online afternoon promo reduces this to €39.

Is golf expensive in Valencia compared to the rest of Spain?

Valencia is among the more affordable golf regions in Spain. Costa del Sol courses average €80–€150 for an 18-hole round. In Valencia, four courses offer verified rates under €70, and the Manises bono works out to €30 per 9-hole round.

Can I play El Saler without booking far in advance?

El Saler is operated by Paradores and can be booked online at paradores.es. The advance rate of €115 is available when booking ahead — rack rate is €150. Tee times can book up during peak season (spring and autumn), so book at least a week in advance.

Do Valencia golf courses require a handicap?

Most courses welcome visitors without a formal handicap certificate, but private clubs like Manises set a maximum handicap of 36.0. See our guide: Do I need a handicap to play golf in Spain?

Are there 9-hole options to reduce cost?

Yes. Foressos Golf and Manises both offer 9-hole green fees. Foressos: €55 (weekday) / €65 (weekend). Manises: €35 (weekday) / €52 (weekend). Club de Golf Ifach is itself a 9-hole course played twice for 18 holes.
