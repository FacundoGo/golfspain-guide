What is MyGolf Spain? Green Fees, Booking, and Whether It's Worth It (2026)

A clear-eyed guide to mygolf.es — how it works, what it costs, and whether it saves you money compared to booking direct.

📅 Updated June 2026 ✅ Verified from mygolf.es ⏱ 4 min read
Quick Answer

MyGolf Spain (mygolf.es) is a free tee time booking platform — not a discount card. You create a free account, search for a course, pick a slot, and pay the green fee set by the club. There is no annual fee. Prices are usually the same as booking direct, with occasional promotional slots worth checking.

If you've searched for golf booking in Spain, you've probably come across mygolf.es. This guide covers exactly what it is, how it compares to booking directly with the club, and whether the occasional promo rate is worth creating an account for.

How MyGolf Spain works

MyGolf operates as a marketplace between golfers and clubs. Courses list their available tee times and set their own prices — MyGolf does not negotiate discounts on your behalf. What it offers is convenience: one platform to compare available times across multiple courses, a digital scorecard feature, and a history of your rounds.

MyGolf green fees vs. booking direct — is there a price difference?

In most cases, prices on MyGolf are identical to the club's official tariff. The platform does not typically charge a booking fee to players. Occasionally, clubs list promotional rates on MyGolf — especially last-minute afternoon slots — that are cheaper than their standard tariff. This is how the platform adds value for golfers.

💡 Practical advice: always check the club's own website first (we publish verified prices for every Valencia course here), then cross-reference with MyGolf for any promotional slots. If the prices match, book wherever is most convenient.

Is MyGolf worth using?

✓ Yes, for convenience. If you are comparing available tee times across multiple courses on the same day, MyGolf is faster than visiting five different club websites. The digital scorecard is a useful bonus.
✗ Not for price savings alone. MyGolf is not a discount card — do not expect to pay less than the club's published rate. The occasional promotional slot is the exception, not the rule.

For golfers playing regularly in one area, booking direct with the club and asking about bonos or monthly memberships will almost always be cheaper than booking through any platform. A 10-round bono at Foressos costs €650 — €65 per round — compared to the €70 standard rate. No booking platform matches that.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyGolf free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and booking tee times on MyGolf is free for players. The green fee itself is charged at the rate set by the club.

Does MyGolf offer cheaper green fees than booking direct?

Not as a rule. Prices on MyGolf are usually identical to the club's official tariff. Occasional promotional rates appear, especially for afternoon slots — worth checking alongside the official website.

Which golf courses in Valencia use MyGolf?

MyGolf has partnerships with courses across Spain. For the Valencia/Alicante region, availability varies by course. Check mygolf.es directly for current listings, or see our verified price pages for each course.

Is there a MyGolf membership card?

No. MyGolf is a booking platform, not a membership or discount card scheme. There is no annual fee and no card to carry.

How is MyGolf different from a golf bono?

A bono is a multi-round voucher purchased directly from a specific club — typically 10 rounds at a discounted per-round rate. MyGolf is a booking platform that works across many clubs. They serve different purposes: MyGolf for flexibility across courses, a bono for regulars at one course.
