Golf Green Fees in Spain: Real 2026 Prices by Course and Region

No vague ranges, no "contact us for pricing" — just verified green fees at real courses, organised by region.

Quick Summary

Spain's cheapest regulation 18-hole green fees start at €39 (Ifach summer afternoon promo, Alicante) and €53 (Cabopino, Costa del Sol). The most expensive is Valderrama at €600, available Mon–Thu only. The widest selection of affordable courses sits between €50 and €100 — which covers the majority of the country.

Prices below are verified June 2026. Buggy inclusion is noted where it applies.

If you're planning a golf trip to Spain and want to know exactly what you'll pay, this is the page you need. How Prices Change: What You Need to Know

Green fees in Spain are rarely flat. Here's what moves the price up or down:

Season

High season on the Costa del Sol and Canaries (October–April) tends to be more expensive — that's when demand peaks. Summer is cheaper, especially July–August in Andalusia when temperatures push 40°C and courses drop rates to fill tee sheets. Valencia is the opposite: summer golf is pleasant and prices stay steady year-round.

Time of day

Early bird slots (before 09:00) and twilight rates (from 14:00–16:00 depending on the course) can be 30–50% cheaper than peak mid-morning times. If your schedule is flexible, always check for twilight pricing — it's the single easiest way to save money.

Buggy

Many prices above include a shared or individual buggy. If you prefer to walk, ask — you may pay less, though some courses require buggies to protect the course. Where the price above includes a buggy, it's noted in the table.

Booking platforms

myGolf.es is Spain's main domestic booking platform for green fees and tournaments, covering hundreds of courses. For international bookers, Golf in Spain aggregates live pricing and allows direct booking. Both are worth checking for last-minute availability before calling the club.
