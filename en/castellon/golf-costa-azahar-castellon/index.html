About Club de Golf Costa de Azahar

Club de Golf Costa de Azahar is a 9-hole course surrounded by pine trees just 50 metres from the beach at the Grao de Castellón. Designed by D. Ángel Pérez de Leza, it is one of the oldest clubs on the Mediterranean coast and a genuinely affordable, easy-going place to play.

Only 6 km from Castellón city centre, it works well as an urban golf option, and the club also has tennis and padel courts plus a golf school with summer courses. Green fees are among the lowest in the region.

How to get there

Club de Golf Costa de Azahar is at the Grao de Castellón, on the seafront just 6 km from Castellón city centre. From Castellón: ~10 minutes. From Valencia: ~1 hour north via the AP-7. From Castellón-Costa Azahar Airport: ~20 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Club de Golf Costa de Azahar?

A 9-hole adult round costs €24 on weekdays and €30 at weekends. 18 holes cost €40 (weekday) or €45 (weekend) for adults. Juniors (under 21) start at €14 for 9 holes. Member's guests get a reduced rate. Among the most affordable green fees on the Mediterranean coast.

Do I need a handicap to play Club de Golf Costa de Azahar?

No specific handicap requirement is published; as an accessible 9-hole course it welcomes all players.

How do I book a tee time at Club de Golf Costa de Azahar?

Book through golfcostadeazahar.com, by phone on +34 964 280 979, or via info@golfcostadeazahar.com.

Is Club de Golf Costa de Azahar suitable for beginners?

Yes — a short, walkable 9-hole layout beside the beach with an on-site golf school makes it ideal for beginners.

What is the nearest airport to Club de Golf Costa de Azahar?

Castellón-Costa Azahar Airport is about 20 minutes away; Valencia Airport is around an hour south.
