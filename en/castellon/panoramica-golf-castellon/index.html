About Panorámica Club de Golf

Panorámica Club de Golf is best known for one distinction: it is the only golf course in Spain designed by Bernhard Langer, the two-time Masters champion and 1995 European Ryder Cup captain. Opened in 1995, the 18-hole, 6,429 m, Par 72 layout sits in the rolling countryside of northern Castellón near Sant Jordi, surrounded by orange groves.

At 6,429 m it is one of the longer courses in the Comunitat Valenciana, and Langer's strategic design combines length with playability — rewarding accurate, thoughtful golf rather than raw power alone. It's an interesting, well-conditioned layout that flies under the radar compared with the better-known Alicante and Valencia courses.

How to get there

Panorámica is near Sant Jordi (San Jorge), Castellón, in the north of the province close to the Tarragona border. From the AP-7 motorway take the Vinaròs/Benicarló exit; the course is a short drive inland. From Castellón-Costa Azahar Airport: ~25 minutes. From Valencia: roughly 1 hour 30 minutes south.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Panorámica Golf?

Indicative 2026 rates are around €45 for 18 holes on a weekday and €50 at weekends and on public holidays. These figures come from booking aggregators — confirm the exact price on the official site when you book.

Who designed Panorámica Golf?

Panorámica was designed by Bernhard Langer and opened in 1995. It is the only golf course in Spain designed by the two-time Masters champion and 1995 European Ryder Cup captain.

How do I book a tee time at Panorámica Golf?

Book through the official website panoramicaclubdegolf.com (the tarifas-y-ofertas section) or by phone on +34 964 493 072.

Is Panorámica Golf suitable for beginners?

At 6,429 m it is one of the longer courses in the Comunitat Valenciana and rewards good ball-striking, but its strategic Langer design remains playable for mid-handicappers. Beginners may find the length demanding.

What is the nearest airport to Panorámica Golf?

Castellón-Costa Azahar Airport is the closest, around 25 minutes away. Valencia Airport is roughly 1 hour 30 minutes south and Reus/Barcelona airports lie to the north.
