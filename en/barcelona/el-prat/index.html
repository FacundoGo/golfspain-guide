About the club

Founded in 1912 and granted Royal status by King Alfonso XIII, Real Club de Golf El Prat is one of Spain's most prestigious golf clubs. Located in Terrassa — 30–35 minutes from Barcelona city centre — it is the only Greg Norman-designed course in Continental Europe and has hosted the Spanish Open ten times, most recently as a DP World Tour venue for the Estrella Damm Catalunya Championship.

The club has 45 holes across three named 18-hole courses: the Pink (Rosa), Yellow (Amarillo), and Green (Verde). Visitors may play the Yellow and Pink courses. The Green course is reserved for members only. Maximum handicap for visitors is 36 for both men and women.

Green course (Verde) — members only

The Verde course (6,210 m) is not available to visitors. Access is restricted to members of Real Club de Golf El Prat only. If you wish to play all three courses, enquire about membership at admisiones@rcgep.com.

Membership

El Prat does not publish membership prices online — all enquiries are handled by the admissions team personally. Available membership types:

  • Individual membership
  • Family membership — includes junior access to MiniAugusta academy
  • Junior / senior formulas
  • 1-year membership with Welcome Pack — available for individuals, couples and families

Based on comparable Barcelona private clubs, expect annual fees of €2,000–€5,000+ plus an entry fee. Contact the admissions office for current figures. Members gain access to all three courses including the Verde.

Getting there

Address: Plans de Bonvilar, 17, 08227 Terrassa (not El Prat de Llobregat — note the name can confuse). About 35 minutes from Barcelona by car on the A-2 or C-58 motorway. ~25 minutes from El Prat Airport. Parking on site is free.

Public transport to Terrassa is possible but slow — driving or taxi strongly recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions — Real Club de Golf El Prat

What are the green fees at Real Club de Golf El Prat in 2026?

Visitor green fees depend on the course and tee time. Yellow course (Amarillo, 6,673 m): €165 (standard), €155 (afternoon), €145 (late afternoon). Pink course (Rosa, 6,492 m): €195. The Green course (Verde) is for members only. Source: open.teeone.golf, verified June 2026.

How many holes does Real Club de Golf El Prat have?

El Prat has 45 holes across three 18-hole courses, all designed by Greg Norman. Pink (Rosa): 6,492 m — ranked 6th best course in Spain by Golf Digest. Yellow (Amarillo): 6,673 m. Green (Verde): 6,210 m (members only). Source: realclubdegolfelprat.com/recorridos.

Is Real Club de Golf El Prat open to visitors?

Yes — visitors can play the Yellow and Pink courses. Advance booking is essential via teeone.golf or by emailing reservations@rcgep.com. Maximum handicap is 36. The Green (Verde) course is members only.

How do I book a tee time at Real Club de Golf El Prat?

Book online at open.teeone.golf/es/prat/disponibilidad. You can also email reservations@rcgep.com or call +34 937 281 000. Walk-ins are rarely available, especially at weekends.
