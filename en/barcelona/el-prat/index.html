About the club

Founded in 1912 and granted Royal status by King Alfonso XIII, Real Club de Golf El Prat is one of Spain's most prestigious golf clubs. Located in Terrassa — about 30–35 minutes from Barcelona city centre — it's the only Greg Norman-designed course in Continental Europe and has hosted the Spanish Open ten times, most recently as a DP World Tour venue for the Estrella Damm Catalunya Championship.

The 45 holes are split into five 9-hole loops combinable into two main 18-hole courses: the OPEN (tournament layout) and BLUE (more accessible). Maximum handicap for visitors is 36 for both men and women.

Membership

El Prat does not publish membership prices online — all enquiries are handled by the admissions team personally. Available membership types:

  • Individual membership
  • Family membership — includes junior access to MiniAugusta academy
  • Junior / senior formulas
  • 1-year membership with Welcome Pack — available for individuals, couples and families

Based on comparable Barcelona private clubs, expect annual fees of €2,000–€5,000+ plus an entry fee. Contact the admissions office for current figures.

Facilities

  • Driving range — 300m, 50 tees (18 covered)
  • Practice areas — putting, pitching, chipping greens + bunkers
  • Restaurant & snack bar
  • Pro shop · Buggy, trolley & club hire
  • Pool area (members and guests)
  • Padel tennis courts
  • Wellness centre & changing rooms
  • Hotel La Mola (185 rooms) — adjacent, Stay & Play packages

Getting there

Address: Plans de Bonvilar, 17, 08227 Terrassa (not El Prat de Llobregat — note the name can confuse). About 35 minutes from Barcelona by car on the A-2 or C-58 motorway. ~25 minutes from El Prat Airport. Parking on site is free.

Public transport to Terrassa is possible but slow — driving or taxi strongly recommended.
