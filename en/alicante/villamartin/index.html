About Villamartín Golf

Villamartín Golf is one of the Costa Blanca Sur's longest-established courses. Designed by Paul Putman and opened in 1972, the 6,005 m, Slope 136 layout has been a benchmark in the Orihuela Costa area for over 50 years. It is part of La Finca Resort, which combines hotel, spa and golf.

Pricing is dynamic and varies based on tee-time availability and how far in advance you book — the earlier you book, the lower the price. The quoted minimum is from €52, though busy periods and peak season will push this higher. Book online to see the exact rate for your date.

How to get there

Villamartín is in Orihuela Costa (Alicante) on the southern Costa Blanca. From Alicante: take the AP-7 motorway towards Murcia, exit for Orihuela Costa/Torrevieja. Drive time from Alicante: ~40 minutes. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~35 minutes.
