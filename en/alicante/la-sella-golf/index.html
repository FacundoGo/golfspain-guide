About La Sella Golf

La Sella Golf is the largest golf complex on the Costa Blanca, with 27 holes arranged as three 9-hole loops — Llebeig, Gregal and Mestral, each named after a Mediterranean wind. Designed by José Canales, the course sits beside the Montgó Natural Park near Dénia, with sea views from all three nines, and has hosted Ladies European Tour events.

The five-star Dénia Marriott La Sella Golf Resort & Spa sits on site, making it a strong play-and-stay base. Green fees are fully dynamic through GolfManager — the earlier you book, the lower the price — so treat the figures below as indicative and confirm the exact rate at booking.

How to get there

La Sella is in Jesús Pobre (Dénia) on the northern Costa Blanca, beside the Montgó Natural Park. From the AP-7 take the Dénia/Ondara exit; ~10 minutes inland. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~1 hour. From Valencia Airport: ~1 hour 15 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at La Sella Golf?

Green fees are dynamic via GolfManager, but expect around €100 for 18 holes and €60 for 9. Book early for the lowest price and confirm the exact rate online.

Do I need a handicap to play La Sella Golf?

Yes — La Sella asks for a maximum handicap of 35. Bring your federation card or licence.

How do I book a tee time at La Sella Golf?

Book online through lasella.golfmanager.com, on lasellagolf.com, or by phone on +34 966 454 252.

Is La Sella Golf suitable for beginners?

With 27 holes and three loops of varying difficulty it suits a range of abilities, though the handicap-35 requirement means it is aimed at established players rather than complete beginners.

What is the nearest airport to La Sella Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about an hour away and Valencia Airport about 1 hour 15 minutes — La Sella sits roughly between the two on the northern Costa Blanca.
