About La Finca Golf

La Finca Golf is one of the most complete golf resorts on the Costa Blanca, anchored by an 18-hole, Par 72 course and a five-star hotel and spa in Algorfa. It belongs to the same group as Villamartín and Las Ramblas, so a stay here gives access to three courses within a few minutes of each other.

Green fees are not published as a fixed tariff — booking is dynamic through the resort's system — so the figure below is an indicative starting point. Book online or contact the resort for the exact rate on your date.

How to get there

La Finca Golf is in Algorfa, inland on the Costa Blanca Sur near the Orihuela Costa. From Alicante: ~40 minutes via the AP-7/A-7. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~35 minutes. Villamartín and Las Ramblas are a short drive away.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at La Finca Golf?

La Finca uses dynamic pricing rather than a fixed tariff, starting from around €50. Book through the resort system for the exact rate on your date.

Do I need a handicap to play La Finca Golf?

No public handicap requirement is stated; as a resort course it welcomes a wide range of players. Confirm when booking.

How do I book a tee time at La Finca Golf?

Book online at lafincaresort.golfmanager.com or contact the resort on +34 966 729 055 / info@lafincaresort.com.

Is La Finca Golf suitable for beginners?

Yes — as a five-star resort course it caters to all levels, with tuition and practice facilities on site.

What is the nearest airport to La Finca Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 35 minutes away on the Costa Blanca Sur.
