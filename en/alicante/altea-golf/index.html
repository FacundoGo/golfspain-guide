About Altea Club de Golf

Altea Club de Golf — formerly Don Cayo Golf — is the only golf course in Altea, set at the foot of the Sierra Bernia mountains on the Costa Blanca Norte. The 9-hole mountain layout dates back to around 1978 and combines elevation changes with sea views, making it one of the most scenic short courses in the area.

It is also one of the most affordable places to play on the northern Costa Blanca, with discounted under-25 rates and good-value multi-round buggy packages. The clubhouse has a restaurant and terrace overlooking the mountains. Altea sits between Benidorm and Calpe, within easy reach of La Sella, Villaitana and Puig Campana for a multi-course trip.

How to get there

Altea Club de Golf is in Altea la Vella, inland from the coastal town of Altea on the Costa Blanca Norte. From Benidorm: ~15 minutes. From Alicante-Elche Airport: about an hour via the AP-7/N-332. From Valencia: roughly 1 hour 30 minutes south.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Altea Club de Golf?

A round of 18 holes (the 9-hole course played twice) costs €49 in low season and €59 in high season; nine holes are €35–€39. Under-25s pay €20 for 9 holes and €30 for 18. Buggy and multi-round packages are also available.

Do I need a handicap to play Altea Club de Golf?

Altea does not publish a handicap requirement. The 9-hole mountain layout is welcoming to higher handicappers, but it is best to confirm any requirement when you book.

How do I book a tee time at Altea Club de Golf?

Book online through the club's booking system at altea.golfmanager.com, on alteaclubdegolf.com, or by phone on +34 966 884 046.

Is Altea Club de Golf suitable for beginners?

Yes. As a 9-hole mountain course with no published handicap requirement and discounted under-25 rates, Altea is one of the more accessible and affordable options on the northern Costa Blanca.

What is the nearest airport to Altea Club de Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about an hour away. Valencia Airport is roughly 1 hour 30 minutes north. Altea sits between Benidorm and Calpe on the Costa Blanca Norte.
