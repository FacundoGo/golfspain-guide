About Puig Campana Golf

Puig Campana Golf is a 9-hole course set at the foot of the Puig Campana, one of the most recognisable peaks on the Costa Blanca. Just ten minutes from Benidorm in Finestrat, it is among the most scenic short courses in the area, with mountain backdrops on every hole and a restaurant and terrace overlooking the valley.

It is an affordable, relaxed place to play, well placed for a multi-course trip alongside Villaitana, Altea and La Sella. Booking is handled through local agents rather than a dedicated website, and prices vary seasonally — treat the rates below as indicative and confirm before you travel.

How to get there

Puig Campana Golf is in Finestrat, just inland from Benidorm on the Costa Blanca. From Benidorm: ~10 minutes. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~45 minutes via the AP-7. It is close to Villaitana and a short drive from Altea.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Puig Campana Golf?

Approximate rates are around €45 in summer / low season rising to roughly €59 in spring and autumn for 18 holes (the 9-hole course played twice). Confirm the current price when booking.

Do I need a handicap to play Puig Campana Golf?

No handicap requirement is published. As a relaxed 9-hole course it is accessible to most players.

How do I book a tee time at Puig Campana Golf?

Booking is through local agents and platforms such as golfenalicante.com rather than a dedicated club website.

Is Puig Campana Golf suitable for beginners?

Yes — its 9-hole layout and easy-going feel make it well suited to beginners and improving players.

What is the nearest airport to Puig Campana Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 45 minutes away. The course is just 10 minutes from Benidorm on the northern Costa Blanca.
