About Real Club de Golf Campoamor

Real Club de Golf Campoamor is one of the most prestigious clubs on the southern Costa Blanca. Opened in 1988 and inaugurated by HRH Juan de Borbón, Count of Barcelona, the 18-hole, 6,277 m, Par 72 layout sits in Dehesa de Campoamor on the Orihuela Costa. A microclimate between the valleys keeps the course playable all year, and every August the club hosts a tournament in honour of the Count of Barcelona.

The club welcomes visitors who hold a valid handicap. Green fees are seasonal, split between high season (March, April, May, October and November) and low season (the rest of the year). The clubhouse is a 2,800 m² Social Club perched 130 m above sea level with sweeping views over the course and the Mediterranean; the four-star Hotel Golf Campoamor I is on site, alongside football pitches and conference facilities.

How to get there

Campoamor is in Dehesa de Campoamor (Orihuela Costa, Alicante) on the southern Costa Blanca, near the border with Murcia. From Alicante: take the AP-7 motorway towards Cartagena/Murcia and exit for Dehesa de Campoamor — about 45 minutes. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~40 minutes. From Murcia-Corvera Airport: ~35 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Real Club de Golf Campoamor?

An 18-hole green fee is €75 in low season and €92 in high season. Nine holes cost €50–€57. Junior, second-round and buggy rates are also available, and the tariffs are valid until 31 December 2026.

Do I need a handicap certificate to play Campoamor?

Yes. Campoamor is a federated club and a valid handicap certificate is required to play the 18-hole course.

How do I book a tee time at Campoamor?

Book through the official website lomasdecampoamor.es or contact the club directly. Visitors are welcome year-round thanks to the course's mild microclimate.

Is Campoamor suitable for beginners?

The 6,277 m, Par 72 layout is a championship-level course best suited to players with an established handicap. Beginners can practise on site, but a handicap is required to play the main course.

What is the nearest airport to Campoamor?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 40 minutes away and Murcia-Corvera Airport about 35 minutes, making Campoamor easy to reach from either side of the Costa Blanca Sur.
