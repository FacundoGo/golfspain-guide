About Las Colinas Golf

Las Colinas Golf & Country Club is one of the highest-rated courses in Spain, regularly featuring in European top-10 lists and named European Golf Course of the Year. The 18-hole Cabell B. Robinson layout runs through a series of secluded valleys on the Orihuela Costa, with sea views and immaculate conditioning.

It is managed by Troon, the operator behind courses such as Spyglass Hill, and although it sits within a private residential community, visitors are warmly welcomed. Green fees are dynamic, starting from around €68 — book online for the exact rate.

How to get there

Las Colinas is in Dehesa de Campoamor, Orihuela Costa, southern Costa Blanca. From Alicante: ~45 minutes via the AP-7 towards Murcia. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~40 minutes; from Murcia-Corvera Airport: ~35 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Las Colinas Golf?

Las Colinas uses dynamic pricing from around €68 for 18 holes. Book online for the exact rate on your chosen date.

Do I need a handicap to play Las Colinas Golf?

A reasonable standard of play is expected at this championship course; bring your handicap card and confirm any requirement when booking.

How do I book a tee time at Las Colinas Golf?

Book online at lascolinas.golfmanager.com or via lascolinasgolf.com.

Is Las Colinas Golf suitable for beginners?

It is a strategic, high-quality championship course rewarding good golf, so it suits mid-handicappers and better rather than complete beginners.

What is the nearest airport to Las Colinas Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 40 minutes away and Murcia-Corvera Airport about 35 minutes.
