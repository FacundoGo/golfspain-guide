About Lo Romero Golf

Lo Romero Golf, near Pilar de la Horadada at the southern tip of the Costa Blanca, is best known for its dramatic island-green 18th hole — “La Isla del Golf.” Opened in 2008, the 6,237 m, Par 72 layout winds through a natural landscape of pine carrasco, carob trees and palms, and is ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified for quality and environmental management.

With an average year-round temperature of around 18°C and a location 40 minutes from both Alicante and Murcia airports, it is a reliable winter-golf option. Pricing is fully dynamic, so last-minute deals are common — book online for the best available rate.

How to get there

Lo Romero Golf is near Pilar de la Horadada at the southern edge of the Costa Blanca, close to the Murcia border. It is roughly 40 minutes from both Alicante-Elche Airport and Murcia-Corvera Airport via the AP-7.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Lo Romero Golf?

Lo Romero uses dynamic pricing from around €50 in summer, rising in peak season. Last-minute deals are common — book online for the live rate.

Do I need a handicap to play Lo Romero Golf?

No fixed handicap requirement is published; bring your card and confirm any requirement when booking.

How do I book a tee time at Lo Romero Golf?

Book online at loromero.golfmanager.com, via loromerogolf.com, or by phone on +34 966 766 887.

Is Lo Romero Golf suitable for beginners?

The course is enjoyable for a range of abilities, though the island-green 18th and 6,237 m length add a sting for higher handicappers.

What is the nearest airport to Lo Romero Golf?

Both Alicante-Elche and Murcia-Corvera airports are about 40 minutes away.
