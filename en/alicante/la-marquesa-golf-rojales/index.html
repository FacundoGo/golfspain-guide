About La Marquesa Golf

La Marquesa Golf is a mature parkland course in Rojales, near the Segura river and the salt lakes of Torrevieja on the southern Costa Blanca. The 6,115 m, Par 72 layout is well established and tree-lined, offering a fair but enjoyable test for visitors and a good base for golfers staying around Torrevieja and Guardamar.

It is relatively affordable for an 18-hole championship course in the area, and sits close to the Murcia border, making it a handy stop on a multi-course trip. Green fees are seasonal, with a 10% discount available through Ruta365.

How to get there

La Marquesa Golf is in Rojales, near Torrevieja on the southern Costa Blanca. From Alicante: ~40 minutes via the AP-7. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~35 minutes; from Murcia-Corvera Airport: ~30 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at La Marquesa Golf?

18H green fees are seasonal: €87 in high season (Mar–May, Sep–Nov) and €80 in mid season (Dec–Feb). Online direct booking in high season costs €83; twilight (after 3PM) €73. Nine holes: €49 (high) / €47 (mid). Junior rate (under 16): €45. A low-season (Jun–Aug) rate of €71 is listed on Ruta365 — confirm current Jun–Aug pricing directly with the club.

Do I need a handicap to play La Marquesa Golf?

No specific handicap requirement is published; it is an accessible parkland course welcoming a wide range of players.

How do I book a tee time at La Marquesa Golf?

Book through lamarquesagolf.es, by phone on +34 966 714 258, or via reservas@lamarquesagolf.es.

Is La Marquesa Golf suitable for beginners?

Yes — its fair, mature parkland layout makes it enjoyable for improving players as well as low handicappers.

What is the nearest airport to La Marquesa Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 35 minutes away and Murcia-Corvera Airport about 30 minutes.
