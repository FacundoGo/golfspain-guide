About Las Ramblas Golf

Las Ramblas Golf is a scenic 18-hole, Par 72 course of 5,914 m on the Orihuela Costa, threading through dry river valleys (ramblas) and natural Mediterranean vegetation. Being shorter than its sister course Villamartín, it is more forgiving and suits mid-to-high handicappers looking for an enjoyable, characterful round.

It is part of La Finca Resort, alongside Villamartín and La Finca Golf, with play-and-stay packages and annual membership from around €2,000. Green fees are dynamic — book online for the exact rate.

How to get there

Las Ramblas Golf is on the Orihuela Costa, southern Costa Blanca. From Alicante: ~40 minutes via the AP-7 towards Murcia. From Alicante-Elche Airport: ~35 minutes. It sits beside Villamartín and La Finca Golf.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Las Ramblas Golf?

Las Ramblas uses dynamic pricing from around €42 for 18 holes. Book through the resort system for the exact rate on your date.

Do I need a handicap to play Las Ramblas Golf?

No public handicap requirement is stated. As the more forgiving of the La Finca courses it welcomes mid-to-high handicappers.

How do I book a tee time at Las Ramblas Golf?

Book online at lafincaresort.golfmanager.com or contact the resort on +34 966 729 055.

Is Las Ramblas Golf suitable for beginners?

Yes — at 5,914 m it is shorter and more accessible than Villamartín, making it a good choice for improving players.

What is the nearest airport to Las Ramblas Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 35 minutes away on the Costa Blanca Sur.
