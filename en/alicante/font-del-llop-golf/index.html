About Font del Llop Golf

Font del Llop Golf Resort sits in Monforte del Cid, just 10 minutes from Alicante International Airport — the most convenient course in the region for fly-in golf. The 6,290 m, Par 72 layout was designed by Blake Stirling & Marco Martin and opened in 2009, blending American design with Scottish-style mounding among vineyards, almond and olive trees.

It is a long and demanding test, with doglegs, bunkering and water hazards throughout, and sits close to Alenda Golf for a Monforte del Cid double-header. Prices are aggregator-confirmed; the official site was not loading at the time of writing, so confirm rates when you book.

How to get there

Font del Llop is in Monforte del Cid, just 10 minutes from Alicante-Elche Airport — the easiest course to reach if you are flying in. From Alicante city: ~20 minutes. It sits next to Alenda Golf, also in Monforte del Cid.

Frequently asked questions

What is the green fee at Font del Llop Golf?

Indicative rates are from €59 for 18 holes, with twilight rounds around €52 and a two-player buggy package near €132. These come from booking platforms — confirm before booking.

Do I need a handicap to play Font del Llop Golf?

No specific handicap requirement is published, but as a 6,290 m championship layout it rewards competent players. Confirm any requirement when booking.

How do I book a tee time at Font del Llop Golf?

Book through CLG (clgolf.es) or contact the pro shop on +34 966 126 767 / info@fontdelllop.com.

Is Font del Llop Golf suitable for beginners?

At 6,290 m with water and bunkering throughout it is fairly demanding, so it suits improving and experienced players more than complete beginners.

What is the nearest airport to Font del Llop Golf?

Alicante-Elche Airport is just 10 minutes away — making Font del Llop the most airport-convenient course in the region.
