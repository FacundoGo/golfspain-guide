How it works

  1. Enter your gross score for each round and select the course played.
  2. We calculate a Handicap Differential: (Score − Course Rating) × (113 ÷ Slope)
  3. We take your best differentials based on how many rounds you've entered (WHS table).
  4. Apply the WHS 96% factor to get your estimated Handicap Index.
Want an official handicap?
This tool gives you an estimate — useful to know which tee boxes to play from and which courses suit your level. For an official WHS Handicap Index, join a golf club affiliated with the Real Federación Española de Golf (RFEG) and register your rounds.
