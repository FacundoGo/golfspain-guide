Useful to know

Golf Membership in Spain — Common Questions

The Spanish golf club system confuses a lot of expats and visitors. Here are the questions we get most.

It varies a lot. Public and semi-private clubs charge roughly €500–€2,000/year. Mid-tier private clubs like Foressos Golf charge from around €600/year with no share required. Top private clubs like Escorpión or Real Club de Golf El Prat require an entry share (acción) worth thousands of euros on top of annual fees — and don't publish prices publicly.
Green fees range from ~€10 for a 9-hole public round (Requena) up to €160+ at top private clubs. The cheapest verified 18-hole visitor rate is €49.50 at Club de Golf Ifach. Premium courses like El Saler charge €115–€150, and Real Club de Golf El Prat in Barcelona charges €108–€156 depending on season.
Most private clubs in Spain welcome visitors with a valid handicap certificate (typically maximum 28–36). Advance booking is essential — walk-ins are rarely accepted. Some clubs like Escorpión are restricted to guests of members or bookings via authorised agencies. Always check the specific club's visitor policy before travelling.
An acción is a membership share required to join certain private Spanish golf clubs as a full socio. It's a one-time capital purchase (separate from annual fees) that can range from a few thousand euros to over €50,000 at the most exclusive clubs. Some clubs allow you to sell your share when you leave. Not all clubs require one — always ask before enquiring about membership.
Valencia offers a strong mix of affordable and premium clubs within a compact area — 8 clubs verified, green fees from €10 to €150. Barcelona & Catalonia has prestige clubs including El Prat (DP World Tour) and PGA Catalunya (Ryder Cup 2031 host). The Costa del Sol has the highest density of international-standard courses. Glossary

Key Terms for Golf in Spain

Spanish golf clubs use terminology that doesn't translate directly. Here's what you'll encounter.

Socio
A full club member, usually paying a monthly or annual fee. Full socios often have priority tee times and voting rights.
Acción
A membership share required to join some private clubs. A one-time capital outlay, separate from annual fees. Can sometimes be resold.
Abono / Cuota
The recurring membership fee — monthly (mensual) or annual (anual). The main ongoing cost of belonging to a club.
Transeúnte
A non-shareholder member who pays higher fees than a full socio but doesn't hold an acción. Common at clubs that allow it.
Green fee
Pay-per-round rate for visitors. Displayed as weekday (laborable) and weekend (fin de semana / festivo).
HCP / Hándicap
Most Spanish clubs require a valid WHS handicap to play. Typically max 28 for men, 36 for women — but varies by club.
