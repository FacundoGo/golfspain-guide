⚠️ Important: federation licence required

Layos Golf requires a valid golf federation licence to play — a standard handicap certificate is not sufficient. Make sure you hold an active licence from the Royal Spanish Golf Federation (RFEG) or an equivalent national federation. International visitors should confirm requirements with the club before booking.

About Layos Golf

Layos Golf was designed by Masters champion José María Olazábal and is widely regarded as one of the most challenging courses in Castilla-La Mancha. With a Course Rating of 75.2 and Slope 135, this is a course built for experienced players — accuracy and course management are rewarded at every hole.

Located in the village of Layos, just 12 km from Toledo city centre and 80 km from Madrid, it makes a natural complement to a visit to the historic city of Toledo or an easy day trip from the capital.

How to get there

Layos is 12 km from Toledo city centre and 80 km from Madrid. From Madrid: take the A-42 motorway towards Toledo, then the CM-40 ring road or local road towards Layos. Drive time from Madrid: ~1h. From Toledo: ~15 minutes by car.
