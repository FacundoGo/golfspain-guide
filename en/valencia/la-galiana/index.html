About La Galiana Golf Resort

Designed by Moon Masters / Felipe Rodríguez and opened in 2011, La Galiana quickly became one of Spain's finest resort courses — winning the World Golf Awards Best Golf Course in Spain 2019. The course is set in the fertile orange-grove landscape of the Ribera Alta, south of Valencia.

La Galiana no longer operates a membership scheme — the club has switched to a fully pay-and-play model. The course is open to the public; book a tee time and play. This is a recent change in the club's business model.

Frequently Asked Questions — La Galiana Golf Resort

What are the green fees at La Galiana Golf in 2026?

18-hole green fees vary by season: €80 in winter (December), €85 in summer (15 Jun–20 Sep), and €105 during shoulder season (Mar–Jun & Sep–Nov). Nine-hole rates are not published — contact the club directly.

Is La Galiana Golf open to visitors?

Yes. La Galiana operates as a fully pay-and-play resort — no membership required. Book a tee time online at ontee.com and play.

Does La Galiana Golf offer membership?

No. La Galiana switched from a membership model to a fully pay-and-play format. There is no annual membership or abono available. If you want a membership club nearby, consider Foressos (from €155/month) or El Bosque (from €1,692/year).

How far is La Galiana Golf from Valencia?

About 50 km south of Valencia city centre, near Carcaixent. Take the A-7 south towards Alzira, then the CV-50 to km 11. Drive time: approximately 45 minutes.

Who designed La Galiana Golf Course?

La Galiana was designed by Moon Masters / Felipe Rodríguez and opened in 2011. It won the World Golf Awards Best Golf Course in Spain award in 2019.
