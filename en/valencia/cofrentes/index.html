About Cofrentes Golf

Cofrentes Golf is a pitch & putt course that opened in 2006 inside the grounds of the Balneario Hervideros de Cofrentes, a thermal spa whose waters were declared of public utility in 1902. It sits in the Ayora-Cofrentes valley, close to where Valencia meets Albacete and Cuenca, and is run by Mediterráneo Golf Management.

The layout is 9 holes, par 27 and 617 metres, and some holes have two tee positions so a second lap plays differently. An 18-hole round is par 54 and 1,234 metres. There are separate chipping and putting greens for practice.

What sets it apart from Masía de las Estrellas or the Manises pitch & putt is the setting: mountain scenery, a spa hotel on site, and night golf with glow-in-the-dark balls. Visitors describe the course as well kept with decent greens, though the bunkers get less attention and the location is remote.

Make it a spa weekend

The Hotel Balneario de Cofrentes is on the same site. Listed packages for two people start at around €150 per night with breakfast and the thermal circuit, and go up to about €215 with full board. A round of pitch & putt can be added from roughly €12 per person.

How to get there

Cofrentes is about 105 km from Valencia city, in the Ayora-Cofrentes valley. Plan on roughly 1 hour 15 minutes by car. There is no practical public transport, so you will need a car. The closest other course in the province is Requena Golf, also inland.

Frequently Asked Questions — Cofrentes Golf

How much is the green fee at Cofrentes Golf in 2026?

Published rates are around €15 on weekdays and €18 at weekends for the pitch & putt. Spa hotel packages add pitch & putt from about €12 per person. We are still confirming 2026 prices directly with the club, so call before you go.

Do you need a handicap to play Cofrentes pitch & putt?

No handicap certificate is needed for a casual round. The course is aimed at beginners and at experienced golfers who want to work on their short game. The club also runs introductory lessons (bautismo de golf).

How long is the Cofrentes golf course?

9 holes, par 27, 617 metres. Played twice for an 18-hole round it is par 54 and 1,234 metres. Some holes have two tee positions.

Can you play golf at night in Cofrentes?

Yes. The club offers night golf using transparent balls lit with a chemical glow stick. Ask the club for dates and prices, as sessions are organised on request.

How far is Cofrentes Golf from Valencia?

About 105 km from Valencia city, in the Ayora-Cofrentes valley near the borders with Albacete and Cuenca. Plan on roughly 1 hour 15 minutes by car. It works best as a spa and golf weekend rather than a quick round.
