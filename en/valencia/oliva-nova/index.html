About Oliva Nova Golf

Oliva Nova Golf is one of the most spectacular courses in the Valencia region. Designed by Seve Ballesteros, the layout makes full use of the coastal landscape: 15 of the 18 holes bring water into play — lakes, rivers and ponds that demand accuracy throughout. The course sits within the Oliva Nova Beach & Golf Resort, minutes from the Costa Blanca Norte beaches.

No membership is required to play — book a tee time online and bring your handicap certificate. Rates vary by season, making it particularly good value in the quieter months.

How to get there

Oliva Nova is in Oliva (Valencia), on the northern Costa Blanca, roughly 100 km south of Valencia city and 90 km north of Alicante. Take the AP-7 or N-332 motorway south from Valencia. Drive time from Valencia: ~1h. From Alicante: ~1h.
