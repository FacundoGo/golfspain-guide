About El Bosque Golf

El Bosque is a well-established members' club near Chiva, west of Valencia. The 18-hole Par 72 course stretches 6,249 metres and offers a classic parkland experience with a strong membership community.

The club operates a dual membership system: socios accionistas (shareholders) pay lower annual fees, while transeúntes (non-shareholders) have full access but pay a higher rate. The acción at El Bosque is more affordable than at clubs like Escorpión, making it accessible to a wider range of players.

Frequently Asked Questions — El Bosque Golf

What are the green fees at El Bosque Golf in 2026?

Pre-DANA tariff: €105 (weekday 18H) / €125 (weekend 18H) / €73 (weekday 9H) / €87 (weekend 9H). El Bosque was seriously affected by the October 2024 DANA floods — contact the club at elbosquegolf.com to confirm current open status and pricing.

Is El Bosque Golf open after the DANA floods?

The course sustained significant damage in the October 2024 floods and has been undergoing reconstruction. Check elbosquegolf.com or call the club directly to confirm current open status before making any trip.

How far is El Bosque Golf from Valencia?

About 30 km west of Valencia city centre, near Chiva. Take the A-3 motorway towards Madrid and exit at Chiva. Drive time from central Valencia: approximately 30 minutes.

How much does El Bosque Golf membership cost?

Pre-DANA tariff: accionista (shareholder) adult from €1,692/year; transeúnte (non-shareholder) adult from €2,712/year. Verify current fees and enrolment status with the club — the DANA recovery may have affected membership arrangements.

Can visitors play El Bosque Golf?

Pre-DANA, visitors could book tee times online via golfmanager. Given the ongoing flood recovery, contact the club at elbosquegolf.com to confirm whether the course is accepting visitors before booking.
