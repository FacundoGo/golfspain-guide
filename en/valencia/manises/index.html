About Real Club de Golf Manises

Real Club de Golf Manises is the closest regulation golf course to Valencia Airport — just 7 km from the terminal, making it the natural first stop for visiting golfers landing in Valencia. With a Slope of 142 and Course Rating of 72.3 from the white tees, this is one of the most technically demanding courses in the area, punching well above its price point.

The club has an 18-hole main course plus a separate Pitch & Putt layout (no handicap required for P&P). Green fees are among the most competitive of any regulation 18-hole course in Valencia: €57 on weekdays and €68 at weekends — significantly cheaper than most comparable courses in the region.

Membership is currently on a waiting list. An entry fee of €4,000 was approved by the club in June 2025, plus annual fees. Contact the club at recepcion@clubgolfmanises.es to join the waiting list.

How to get there

Real Club de Golf Manises is 7 km from Valencia Airport — the closest regulation golf course to the terminal. From the airport, it's approximately 10 minutes by car or taxi. From Valencia city centre: 15–20 minutes via the V-30 or A-3 motorway. Manises is directly west of the city.
