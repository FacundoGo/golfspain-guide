About Foressos Golf

Foressos is one of the most welcoming clubs in Valencia for newcomers. The 18-hole Par 72 course runs through varied terrain south of Valencia near Picassent, easily reached from the A7 motorway.

What sets Foressos apart is its no-entry-fee, monthly abono model. No share purchase, no large upfront joining fee — just start a monthly direct debit. Family-friendly too: children under 17 play free when a parent is a member.

Frequently Asked Questions — Foressos Golf

What are the green fees at Foressos Golf in 2026?

Visitor green fees: €70 (weekday 18H), €80 (weekend 18H), €55 (weekday 9H), €65 (weekend 9H). Twilight (from 17:00): €35 for 9 holes. Best-value package: 2 green fees + buggy for €119 (weekday).

Is Foressos Golf open to visitors?

Yes. Visitors are welcome without a membership. Book online at ontee.com. No handicap certificate is required for casual play.

Does Foressos Golf offer a 10-round bono?

There is no formal 10-round bono listed. The best deal for repeat visitors is the 2 green fees + 1 buggy package for €119 (weekday, 18 holes). For regular play, the afternoon membership from €155/month works out considerably cheaper.

How much does Foressos Golf membership cost?

Membership has no entry fee. Options start from €50/month (juniors under 18) and €155/month (adults, afternoon access from 13:00). Full 7-day access is €205/month. Under-17s are free when a parent is a member.

How far is Foressos Golf from Valencia?

About 20 km south of Valencia city centre, near Picassent. Exit the A7/N340 at Junction 358. Drive time from central Valencia: 20–25 minutes.
