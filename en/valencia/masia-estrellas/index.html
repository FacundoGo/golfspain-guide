About Masia de las Estrellas

Masia de las Estrellas is a pitch & putt course set in the hills of the Costa Blanca near Benissa. Pitch & putt means shorter holes (typically under 90m) — no handicap certificate required, and playable by beginners and experienced golfers alike. It's a community-oriented club with a relaxed, informal atmosphere entirely different from the formal private clubs found elsewhere in the region.

The course offers exceptional value: €24 for 18 holes, and just €8 to use all the practice facilities (driving range, putting green and mini pitch & putt area). Annual membership is €800/year with no share purchase required, making it one of the most affordable full memberships on the Costa Blanca.

Located near Benissa on the Costa Blanca, between Calpe and Benissa. Approximately 100 km south of Valencia along the AP-7 motorway. Drive time from Valencia: ~1 hour.
