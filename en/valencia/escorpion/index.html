About Club de Golf Escorpión

Founded in 1969 and designed by American architect Ron Kirby, Escorpión is widely regarded as the most exclusive golf club in the Valencia region. Set in the rolling hills of Bétera, the club offers 27 holes across three distinct 9-hole loops, giving members three different 18-hole combinations.

Membership is limited and the acción (club share) holds its value reasonably well. The club has a strong social scene, excellent facilities, and consistent demand from new members.

How to get there

Escorpión is in Bétera, ~20 km north of Valencia. Take the V-21 motorway north, exit towards Bétera or San Antonio de Benagéber. Drive time from central Valencia: ~25–30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions — Club de Golf Escorpión

What does Club de Golf Escorpión membership cost in 2026?

Approximately €20,000 for the club share (acción) plus around €1,500/year in annual fees — a total of about €21,500 in year one. The share can typically be resold when you leave. Contact the club for exact current figures, as these are not published officially.

Is Club de Golf Escorpión open to visitors?

Escorpión is a private members' club. Visitors are typically accepted on weekdays only and usually need an introduction from a member. Weekday rounds can be booked via golfinspain.com.

How many holes does Escorpión Golf have?

27 holes across three distinct 9-hole loops (Pinos, Naranjos and Almendros), giving members three different 18-hole combinations. Designed by Ron Kirby and opened in 1969.

How far is Escorpión Golf from Valencia?

About 20 km north of Valencia, in Bétera. Take the V-21 motorway north. Drive time from central Valencia: approximately 25–30 minutes.

What is the visitor green fee at Escorpión?

Approximately €160 for 18 holes, typically with a member introduction. Contact the club directly to confirm current pricing and to arrange access.
