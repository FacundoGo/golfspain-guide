About Ifach Golf

Ifach Golf is a full-facility private club set in the Urb. San Jaime urbanisation in Benissa, on the Costa Blanca. Named after the iconic Peñón de Ifach rock formation nearby, the club offers far more than just golf — there's a pool, solarium, three tennis courts, a renovated restaurant (Kosta D'Oro, serving Mediterranean, Italian and Japanese cuisine), bar, pro shop and extensive practice facilities.

The 9-hole course is designed for play as both a 9-hole and a full 18-hole round. Membership requires a transferable título de uso y disfrute (share), plus an admission fee and semester dues — but the club is actively open to new members, and member green fees are just €18 for 18 holes.

Membership ✅

✅ Open to new members.

Membership at Ifach Golf requires purchasing a título de uso y disfrute — a transferable share that can be resold if you leave. On top of the share there is a non-refundable admission fee and semester membership dues.

The main benefit: member green fees are just €18 for 18 holes (vs €49.50 for visitors) — a 64% saving. Frequent players will recoup the membership cost quickly.

Exact share prices and semester fees are not published online — contact the club directly.

How to get there

Located in the Urb. San Jaime urbanisation in Benissa, Costa Blanca. Approximately 100 km south of Valencia along the AP-7 motorway — exit at Benissa/Calpe. Drive time from Valencia: ~1 hour. From Alicante: ~45 minutes.
