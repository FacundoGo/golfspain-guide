About Ifach Golf

Ifach Golf is located at the Masia de las Estrellas estate in Benissa, on the Costa Blanca coast between Calpe and Benissa. The 9-hole course sits in a scenic inland setting with views towards the distinctive Peñón de Ifach rock formation.

It's a welcoming, community-run club with a relaxed atmosphere — the opposite of the formal private club. You can play a full 18-hole round (two loops of the 9) for just €24, making it one of the most accessible courses in the region. The facilities include a driving range, putting green, and a mini pitch & putt, all accessible for €8.
