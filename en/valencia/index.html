Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to play a full round of golf in Valencia?

It depends on what you mean by a "full round". The cheapest Pitch & Putt is Masía de las Estrellas (Catarroja) at €24 for 18 holes — no handicap required, but it's a Par 3 course, not a regulation layout.

For a regulation 18-hole course, the cheapest verified rate is Foressos Golf at €70 weekday / €80 weekend.

If budget is the top priority, Requena Golf offers 9 holes for €10 (partnership rate for Masía members) — the cheapest single round in the province.

Which courses are open to any visitor without a membership?

Requena, Ifach, Foressos, La Galiana, El Bosque and El Saler all accept visitors for a green fee. Escorpión requires a member introduction or booking through an authorised agency for guests. El Bosque is operating post-DANA but verify conditions before booking.

What is the difference between a regulation course and Pitch & Putt?

A regulation course has 18 holes of varying length — par 3s, 4s and 5s — and requires a handicap certificate at most clubs. A Pitch & Putt course has 9 short Par 3 holes, played twice for an 18-hole round. No handicap is needed, scores are not WHS-eligible, and differentials are not comparable. Masía de las Estrellas is a Pitch & Putt — great for beginners or a quick game, but not a substitute for a regulation round.

What does "acción" mean in Spanish golf clubs?

An acción is a share or equity stake in a private club. Clubs like Escorpión require you to purchase one (typically €15,000–€25,000) to become a member. It can sometimes be resold when you leave.

Is El Saler really one of the best courses in Europe?

It consistently ranks in Top 5–10 Europe lists. Designed by Javier Arana in 1968, it runs through the Albufera Natural Park. If you play one round in Valencia, this is the one.

Can I become a member at Ifach Golf?

Yes — Ifach is open to new members. Membership requires purchasing a título de uso y disfrute (transferable share). As a member you pay just €18 per round vs €49.50 as a visitor — a 64% saving. Contact the club at +34 966 497 114 or info@golfifach.com.

Can I become a member at Masía de las Estrellas?

There is currently a waiting list. The annual abono is €800/yr with no share purchase required — one of the most affordable full memberships near Valencia. Contact the club to register your interest; you can play as a visitor for €24 in the meantime.
