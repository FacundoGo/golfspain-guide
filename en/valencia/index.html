Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to play 18 holes in Valencia?

Masia de las Estrellas in Benissa offers 18 holes for €24 — the best-value full round in the region. Ifach Golf (also in Benissa) is €49.50 for visitors. For 9 holes on a tight budget, Requena Golf Club is as low as €10 for members of other clubs.

Which courses are open to any visitor without a membership?

Requena, Ifach, Foressos, La Galiana, El Bosque and El Saler all accept visitors for a green fee. Escorpión requires a member introduction for guests.

What does "acción" mean in Spanish golf clubs?

An acción is a share or equity stake in a private club. Clubs like Escorpión require you to purchase one (typically €15,000–€25,000) to become a member. It can sometimes be resold when you leave.

Is El Saler really one of the best courses in Europe?

It consistently ranks in Top 5–10 Europe lists. Designed by Javier Arana in 1968, it runs through the Albufera Natural Park. If you play one round in Valencia, this is the one.

Can I become a member at Ifach Golf?

Yes — Ifach is open to new members. Membership requires purchasing a título de uso y disfrute (transferable share). As a member you pay just €18 per round vs €49.50 as a visitor — a 64% saving. Contact the club at +34 966 497 114 or info@golfifach.com.

Can I become a member at Masia de las Estrellas?

There is currently a waiting list. The annual abono is €800/yr with no share purchase required — one of the most affordable full memberships on the Costa Blanca. Contact the club to register your interest; you can play as a visitor for €24 in the meantime.
