About El Saler

El Saler is widely considered the best golf course in Spain and one of the great links-style courses of Europe. Designed by Javier Arana and opened in 1968, it occupies a stretch of land between the Mediterranean and the Albufera Natural Park. The course alternates between forested inland holes and exposed dune holes that test your game in the coastal wind.

Managed by Paradores de Turismo, Spain's state-owned luxury hotel network, it remains accessible to the public — no membership required to play, just a tee time and your handicap certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions — El Saler Golf

What are the green fees at El Saler in 2026?

A guest of a member pays €60 — verified by a current member. The advance visitor green fee is €115 (book via paradores.es or golfenparadores.es). Rack rate: €150. Verified June 2026.

Is El Saler Golf open to visitors without membership?

Yes. El Saler is a public Paradores course — no membership is required. Anyone can book a tee time online at paradores.es or golfenparadores.es. Advance rate: €115.

Do you need a handicap certificate to play El Saler?

Yes. A valid handicap certificate is required. El Saler is a championship-level course ranked Top 5 in Europe, and the club expects all players to be competent golfers.

How far is El Saler from Valencia city?

About 15 km south of Valencia city centre, inside the Albufera Natural Park. Drive time is approximately 20 minutes via the V-15 or CV-500.

How much does El Saler membership cost?

El Saler offers annual passes rather than traditional membership. Under-30s: approximately €2,400/year. Over-30s: approximately €4,500/year. Figures confirmed by a current member — verify directly with the club before joining.
