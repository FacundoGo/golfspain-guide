Privacy Policy

What data we collect, why, and how to exercise your rights.

Who is responsible for your data

golfspain.guide, Valencia, Spain. Contact for anything related to your data: contact@golfspain.guide.

What we collect and why

  • Newsletter. Your email address, and if you sign up from the handicap calculator, your estimated handicap index, the number of rounds you entered and your language. We use it to send the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent. Kept until you unsubscribe (link in every email).
  • Golf trip plan form. Your name, email, travel dates, number of players, the courses you are interested in and whether you will bring your own clubs. We use it to prepare your plan and, if you ask, to contact you about that trip. Legal basis: your consent. Kept for up to 2 years, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics. Only if you accept cookies: how pages are used, device and browser type and approximate location, through Google Analytics and Hotjar. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • On your device only. The handicap calculator saves your rounds, and the site saves your cookie choice, in your browser's local storage. This data is not sent to us; clear it from your browser at any time.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • beehiiv: newsletter delivery.
  • Google: Google Sheets (trip plan requests), Google Analytics, Google Maps (maps on course pages) and Google Fonts.
  • Hotjar: anonymous usage analytics, only with your consent.
  • Vercel: website hosting, which keeps technical server logs.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy of it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email contact@golfspain.guide and we will reply within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
