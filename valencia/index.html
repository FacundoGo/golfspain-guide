Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to play golf in Valencia?

For occasional play, Requena Golf Club offers green fees as low as €10 for members of other clubs. For regular play, a junior membership at Foressos (€50/month for under-18s) is the most affordable ongoing option.

What does "acción" mean in Spanish golf clubs?

An acción is a share or equity stake in a private club. Clubs like Escorpión require you to purchase one (typically €15,000–€25,000) to become a member. It can sometimes be resold when you leave, so it's partially an investment — but this varies by club.

Can I join a club as an expat or non-resident?

Yes — all clubs listed as "open" accept foreign nationals. You'll need a valid handicap certificate from your home golf federation and a Spanish bank account for the direct debit. Some clubs require a member to sponsor you; we note this in each club's page.

Is El Saler really one of the best courses in Europe?

It consistently ranks in Top 5–10 Europe lists and Top 100 worldwide. Designed by Javier Arana in 1968, it runs through the Albufera Natural Park with dunes and pines. If you play one round in Valencia, this is the one.
