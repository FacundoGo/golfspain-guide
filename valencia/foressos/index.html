About Foressos Golf

Foressos is one of the most welcoming clubs in Valencia for newcomers and those new to golf in Spain. Designed by acclaimed architect José Gancedo, the 18-hole Par 72 course runs through varied terrain south of Valencia city near Picassent, easily reached from the A7 motorway.

What sets Foressos apart is its no-entry-fee, monthly abono model. Unlike clubs that demand a share purchase or a large upfront joining fee, Foressos lets you start a monthly membership with no capital commitment. Family-friendly too — children under 17 play free when a parent is a member.

How to get there

Take the A7 / N340 motorway and exit at Junction 358 (Salida 358). The club is clearly signposted from the slip road. Located approximately 20 km south of Valencia city centre. Drive time from central Valencia: approximately 20–25 minutes.
