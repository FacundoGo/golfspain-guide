About El Saler

El Saler is widely considered the best golf course in Spain and one of the great links-style courses of Europe. Designed by legendary architect Javier Arana and opened in 1968, it occupies a unique stretch of land between the Mediterranean and the Albufera Natural Park — a UNESCO-listed lagoon ecosystem. The course alternates between forested inland holes and exposed dune holes that test your game in the coastal wind.

Unlike most top-tier courses, El Saler is managed by Paradores de Turismo, Spain's state-owned luxury hotel network, which means it remains accessible to the public — no membership required to play, just a tee time and your handicap certificate.

How to get there

El Saler is located 15 km south of Valencia city centre, inside the Albufera Natural Park. Take the V-15 or CV-500 heading south along the coast. The course is clearly signposted from El Saler village. Approximate drive from central Valencia: 20 minutes.

There is no direct public transport to the course — a car or taxi is the practical option.
