About El Bosque Golf

El Bosque is a well-established members' club situated in the countryside near Chiva, west of Valencia. The 18-hole Par 72 course stretches 6,249 metres and offers a classic parkland experience with a strong membership community.

The club operates a dual membership system common in Spanish golf: socios accionistas (shareholders who own a stake in the club) pay lower annual fees, while transeúntes (non-shareholders) have full access but pay a higher annual rate. Unlike Escorpión, the acción at El Bosque is more affordable, making it accessible to a wider range of players.

How to get there

El Bosque is located near Chiva, approximately 30 km west of Valencia. Take the A-3 motorway towards Madrid and exit at Chiva. Drive time from central Valencia: approximately 30 minutes.

💡 Accionista vs Transeúnte

Accionistas own a share in the club and pay lower annual fees. Transeúntes get full access without a share purchase but pay ~€1,000 more per year. Over 5 years, buying a share usually pays off — ask the club about current share availability and price.
