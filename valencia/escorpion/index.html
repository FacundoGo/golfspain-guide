About Club de Golf Escorpión

Founded in 1969 and designed by American architect Ron Kirby, Escorpión is widely regarded as the most exclusive golf club in the Valencia region. Set in the rolling hills of Bétera, north of Valencia, the club offers 27 holes across three distinct 9-hole loops — giving members three different 18-hole combinations.

Membership is limited and demand is consistent, which means the acción (club share) holds its value reasonably well over time. The club has a strong social scene, excellent facilities, and a waiting list mentality despite technically being open to new members.

How membership works — the acción system

Important: To become a member of Escorpión you must first purchase an acción — a share in the club. This is a one-off capital cost, separate from and in addition to the annual membership fee.

The acción is owned by you and can typically be resold if you leave the club — though the resale price depends on market demand at the time and is not guaranteed. Think of it as a deposit that may or may not be recovered.

How to get there

Escorpión is located in Bétera, approximately 20 km north of Valencia city centre. Take the V-21 motorway north, exit towards Bétera or San Antonio de Benagéber, and follow the signs. Drive time from central Valencia: approximately 25–30 minutes.

💡 Understanding the acción

The share purchase is the main barrier to entry at Escorpión. Some members buy a second-hand acción from a departing member at a negotiated price — worth asking the club about this option.
