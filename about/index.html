About golfspain.guide

An independent, no-affiliate guide to golf club membership and green fees across Spain. Built because the information simply didn't exist anywhere else in one place.

Home›About

Why this site exists

If you've ever tried to find out how much it costs to join a golf club in Spain, you'll know the problem: the information is either buried in a PDF on a 2012-era website, missing entirely, or you have to call the club and wait for a callback.

Existing directories like rfegolf.es list every club in the country but show zero pricing. Sites like golfvalencia.es have the right idea but haven't been meaningfully updated in years and still show no fees.

golfspain.guide exists to fix that. We research, call clubs directly, verify PDFs, and publish what we find — with a visible date so you know how fresh the data is.

How we collect data

📞

Direct calls to clubs

The most reliable method. We call reception and ask for current membership tariffs. Some clubs email us their official PDF.

📄

Official tariff documents

Where clubs publish their fee schedules — as PDFs on their own websites — we reference those directly and note the document date.

🔍

Cross-referencing

We check club websites, Google reviews, and golf forums to triangulate pricing when primary sources aren't available.

📅

Visible verification dates

Every price on this site shows when it was last verified. If the date is old, treat it as indicative and call the club to confirm.

What we are not

  • Not affiliated with any club. We don't receive payment from clubs to list them or to rank them higher.
  • Not a booking platform. We link to clubs' own websites for tee time bookings — we don't take a commission.
  • Not always up to date. Golf clubs change their fees. We do our best to verify regularly, but always confirm directly with the club before making a financial decision.

Contact us / corrections

Found an error? Has a club changed its fees? Is a club listed as closed when it's actually open? We want to know.

We also welcome tips about clubs not yet listed — especially membership pricing data from clubs in regions we haven't covered yet.

To report a correction or send data: use the contact form on our GitHub repository or reach us via the social links below. We aim to review and update within 48 hours.
